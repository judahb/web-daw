🎵 Web DAW

Cloud-Hosted Digital Audio Workstation with VST Support

Features

  • Audio and MIDI tracks with user-selectable count
  • Fixed stereo master bus
  • Volume and panning controls per track
  • Transport controls (Play/Pause/Stop)
  • Integrated sequencer and mixer
  • VST instrument support for MIDI tracks
  • VST effects support for audio tracks
  • Remote plugin loading with browser cache
  • AudioGridder client integration
  • Low-latency audio processing
  • Open-source VST support
